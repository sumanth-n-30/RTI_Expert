Potential Rejection Risks
1
Request for personal names — Seeking individual names alongside financial data may trigger exemption under Section 8(1)(j) of the RTI Act (personal information). Consider requesting aggregate data or anonymized records instead.
2
Broad scope — Asking for "all registered beneficiaries" without a specific count cap can lead to a voluminous-request rejection. Adding a time-bound or ward-level filter improves success rate.
3
Jurisdiction clarity — Ensure the application is addressed to the correct PIO at the district MGNREGS office, not a state-level authority, to avoid a jurisdictional transfer.

To,
The Public Information Officer,
District Programme Coordinator, MGNREGS,
Nilgiris District, Tamil Nadu.

Subject: Request for information under the Right to Information Act, 2005 — MGNREGS implementation in Kotagiri Gram Panchayat (FY 2023–24)

I, the undersigned, hereby request the following information pertaining to Gram Panchayat Kotagiri, Nilgiris district, for Financial Year 2023–24:

(a) Total number of job cards issued and the ward-wise breakdown of active beneficiaries, including individual names and job card numbers;
(b) Category-wise aggregate of number of days worked per beneficiary group;
(c) Total wages disbursed, payment mode (cash/DBT), and ward-total of any pending dues as on 31 March 2024.

The information may be provided in the form of a certified register extract or official report.

Key RTI Act provisions to know
§3
Right to information — Every citizen has the right to request information from a public authority. The PIO must respond within 30 days (48 hours for life/liberty matters).
§8
Exemptions — Information affecting sovereignty, privacy (personal info), trade secrets, cabinet deliberations, and ongoing investigations may be withheld.
§19
Appeals — First appeal lies with the First Appellate Authority within 30 days of rejection. Second appeal goes to the Central/State Information Commission.
Tips for stronger RTI applications
Be specificAsk for documents, records, or data — not opinions or reasons.
Avoid personal namesRequest aggregate or anonymized data to bypass Section 8(1)(j).
Narrow the time rangeLimit to one financial year or quarter to prevent voluminous-request rejection.
Address the right PIOConfirm the correct public authority before filing — wrong address causes transfer delays.
Ask for certified copiesPhrasing as "certified copies of records" carries more legal weight than "information about".
Attach fee proof₹10 application fee (IPO/DD/online). BPL applicants are exempt — attach BPL card copy.
